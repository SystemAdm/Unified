🎯 How to Access the Menu in Narrow View:

Step 1: Look for the hamburger menu button (three horizontal lines) in the top-left corner of the header.
Step 2: Click the hamburger menu button to open the sidebar menu.
Step 3: The menu will slide out from the left side, showing all navigation options.
Step 4: Click outside the menu or click the hamburger button again to close it.

📱 When does the hamburger menu appear?

  • Below 760px width: Hamburger menu is visible (mobile mode)
  • Above 760px width: Full sidebar is visible (desktop mode)

🔧 Technical Details:

The menu trigger is implemented using the SidebarTrigger component with a PanelLeft icon. It's always present in the header but becomes the primary navigation method in narrow views.
